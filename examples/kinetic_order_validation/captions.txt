Kinetic-order dependence of band-energy RMS errors for the six original valley models. The upper row uses the manuscript alignment and the lower row uses raw energies. Both rows use the same logarithmic scale within each column. Validation uses the same 61 momentum points and band windows of 8, 8, 10, 6, 3, and 8 bands. ZrS2 M uses the conduction-band edge. The other models use the valence-band edge.

The linear series use no extra matrix weighting or weights (2.25, 22.5) with 6- or 20-state windows. A 20-state request uses all 18 states for the trilayer K_B model. The nonlinear series start from the corresponding six-state linear model and add an eigenvalue loss with weight 1 or 10. The nonlinear loss requests ten bands and keeps complete degenerate groups at the window boundary, giving 10–12 states for K_B. The linear series cover orders 4, 6, 8, and 10. The nonlinear series also include order 2.

The matrix samples are those of the original configurations. Nonlinear refinement uses even-indexed path points subject to the degeneracy check. Seven of the 30 runs with band weight 1 and 16 of the 30 runs with band weight 10 reached the evaluation budget of 30. Stars mark the settings used in the manuscript.
