# Three Visual Directions for the Topological Moire Bands Talk

## Objective

Produce three complete English 16:9 presentations for a 20-minute slot including questions. The three decks share the same audited scientific story and source data, but each must have a genuinely different visual language, layout rhythm, and validation-slide treatment.

## Shared scientific story

The main talk follows `map → select → construct → validate` in eleven slides, followed by two technical backup slides.

1. From a moire-band atlas to reusable continuum Hamiltonians.
2. One twist creates two tasks: search the landscape and model the selected candidate.
3. The high-throughput hierarchy: 43 monolayers, 91 bilayer prototypes, more than 1,000 angle-resolved calculations.
4. Parent-valley character organizes the atlas.
5. Gamma, K, and M valleys follow different reconstruction rules.
6. BiTeCl: termination selects the valley; stacking selects topology.
7. The atlas identifies candidates, but downstream theory needs an explicit matrix Hamiltonian.
8. Matching eigenvalues is not enough because eigenvectors carry topology and interaction matrix elements.
9. MoireKP constructs a symmetry-fixed low-energy matrix model.
10. AA-MoTe2 at K: target-band agreement and a transparent accuracy ladder.
11. The construction adapts across K, Gamma, and M valleys, followed by questions.

Backup slides contain the full eight-band validation and the low-energy-basis derivation. The draft runtime benchmark is excluded.

## Scientific boundaries

- Atlas statistics, valley trends, BiTeCl topology, and screening quantities come from the arXiv manuscript and supplement.
- The MoTe2 validation uses only `paper/moirekp/code_paper/figure_sources/model_tapw_summary/data/mote2_k/band.npz`.
- The visible validation reports target-band RMS `0.35 meV`, top-three RMS `0.41 meV`, top-eight RMS `0.95 meV`, and top-eight maximum absolute error `2.11 meV`, using the documented independent top-band alignment.
- Current TAPW topology-field panels are `tapw_proxy_from_model`; no independent Berry-curvature or quantum-metric validation claim is permitted.
- AI-generated imagery is conceptual only. It may not contain numerical data, fake plots, fake heatmaps, or exact scientific labels.

## Quality gates

- One assertion-style message per slide.
- No copied multi-panel paper figure may be used without deliberate cropping and direct annotation.
- No persistent title rail, sidebar, numbered corporate list, bottom takeaway bar, or pastel card grid.
- Visible text must remain readable at lecture-room scale; captions are at least 11 pt.
- Each deck is rendered to PDF and slide PNGs, reviewed independently, corrected, and rerendered.
- Final outputs remain separate and are not permitted to differ only by palette.
